## Findings and gate decision

- **RENFAST:** The official record describes manually annotated renal fibrosis on 300 Masson's trichrome images and vessel segmentation on 350 PAS images. The local archive has manual fibrosis and tissue masks, and manual vessel masks. It contains 2,350 entries; the fibrosis training split has 500 image entries but only 250 unique normalized basenames, with 250 additional colliding entries. The official licence field is blank, and no local terms file exists. Patient count and physical image resolution are not documented by the reviewed source.
- **KPMP:** The local Atlas snapshot lists 152 WSI and 12 segmentation-mask/pathomics records, all marked open, across the 28-participant development cohort. The local cohort has 33 TRI slides; 12 segmentation packages across 11 participants annotate PAS slides. Package documentation says the segmentation outputs are generated by a deep-learning network and quality-controlled by a renal pathologist. Six package metadata inventories list an IFTA channel, but the v2 README contradicts some channel metadata. No direct expert-drawn collagen mask or verified numeric fibrosis score was identified.
- **Stain and linkage:** RENFAST fibrosis labels are on Masson's trichrome, not H&E. They are not molecularly linked to KPMP. KPMP masks match their packaged PAS sources, not the participant's TRI slide identifiers/dimensions; participant identity does not establish cross-section alignment.
- **Decision:** Stop. RENFAST reuse terms are unresolved, and the KPMP masks cannot be represented as direct expert ground truth. This follows the preregistered stop condition and P8/P10; no surrogate labels or alternative datasets were substituted. Do not proceed to Stage 2B or later stages until this blocker is resolved.
