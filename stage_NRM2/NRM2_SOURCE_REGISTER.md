## Hard requirements

A source was eligible only if all of these were documented:

1. Stain explicitly stated as trichrome, Masson, or Picrosirius.
2. Tissue explicitly labelled normal, healthy, or reference, with the source stating or quoting how the label was assigned.
3. Licence or terms readable and quoted verbatim.
4. No access request, registration, agreement, payment, or institutional approval.
5. Public data suitable for research use.

No source met all five requirements.

## TCGA-KIRC: NOT SUITABLE

- **URL:** [TCGA-KIRC project](https://portal.gdc.cancer.gov/projects/TCGA-KIRC)
- **Stain:** GDC `Slide Image` metadata and the API fields inspected on 2026-11-18 do not state a stain. The returned names identify SVS whole-slide images. This is not a source-documented trichrome, Masson, or Picrosirius stain.
- **Normal label:** GDC field `cases.samples.sample_type` has exact value `Solid Tissue Normal`. This is a cancer-cohort biospecimen code, not a documented healthy or reference label. The source did not establish tumour-adjacent versus other tissue origin.
- **Disease label:** GDC exact field/value `cases.samples.sample_type = Primary Tumor` is tumour tissue, not a fibrosis comparison group.
- **Licence/access wording quoted:** “No login is required when accessing open access data.” NCI also says: “This data, which has already led to improvements in our ability to diagnose, treat, and prevent cancer, will remain publicly available for anyone in the research community to use.”
- **Verdict:** NOT SUITABLE. TCGA-KIRC is removed from the plan; the compatible-stain and true-normal-origin gates fail.

## Human Protein Atlas normal kidney: NOT SUITABLE

- **URL:** [HPA normal kidney](https://www.proteinatlas.org/learn/dictionary/normal/kidney)
- **Stain quote:** “The multiplex images in the dictionary have been generated by highly multiplexed staining of the HPA tissue microarrays using oligonucleotide-barcoded antibodies.”
- **Normal label:** The exact page heading is “Normal tissue Kidney.” The page does not state or quote how the normal label was assigned or establish that the source is a healthy-donor cohort.
- **Licence quote:** “The Human Protein Atlas is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License for all copyrightable parts of our database. Data subject to third-party constraints may also be included and it is the responsibility of users of the Human Protein Atlas to ensure that their utilization of the data does not infringe any of the rights of such third parties.”
- **Verdict:** NOT SUITABLE. It is multiplex antibody staining, not trichrome/Masson/Picrosirius, and normal-label origin is unresolved.
