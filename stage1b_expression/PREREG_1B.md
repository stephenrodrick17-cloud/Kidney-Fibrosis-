## Why Stage 1b exists

Stage 1 reported Gate A FAIL. The learned baselines were limited by pre-result runtime reductions (Elastic Net capped at 10 SGD epochs and MLP at 5 epochs), so this follow-up evaluates converged Elastic Net fits and an MLP with grouped early stopping. The depth-only comparison is retained as a scientific benchmark, but depth/library size is not an image-derived feature and is not assumed to be available at image-only inference. Stage 1b is exploratory and post-hoc; it cannot reverse Gate A or establish confirmatory evidence.

## Hard configuration budget

- **Encoders:** one. Reuse Stage 1's cached, frozen torchvision ResNet-50 `IMAGENET1K_V2` spot embeddings only. Do not extract another encoder or use the saved wider-context embeddings. The torchvision code is BSD-3-Clause; weight-specific and upstream ImageNet dataset terms were not independently verified. No second encoder is attempted.
- **Target types:** exactly two:
  1. `depth_residualized`: in each outer or inner training split, regress each normalized target on `log1p(total spot UMI)` using training spots only and participant-balanced weights; subtract that fitted relationship from train and validation/test targets. Center training residuals within each training participant, as in Stage 1. Do not center held-out participants using their labels.
  2. `depth_residualized_neighbor_mean`: apply the same split-specific residualization, then for each spot average the residualized values of its available immediate in-tissue Visium neighbors in the same library, excluding the spot itself. Immediate neighbors are the six array-coordinate offsets `(0, ±2)`, `(±1, ±1)`. Do not cross library boundaries or impute a value when no immediate neighbor is available; list such omissions.
- **Model families:** exactly two learned image families: Elastic Net and a one-hidden-layer MLP. The only image inputs are (a) the Stage 1 spot embedding and (b) that embedding concatenated with `log1p(total spot UMI)`. Fixed patient-mean, depth-only Ridge, and colour-only Ridge are comparison baselines, not additional tuned image-model families.
- **Outer evaluation:** reuse the exact seven participant-grouped Stage 1 folds and spot identity `(participant_id, library_id, barcode)`. No participant or library is reassigned. Fit all preprocessing and target residualization within the applicable training split.
- **Elastic Net:** `l1_ratio=0.5`; fixed alpha grid `[0.001, 0.01, 0.1]`. Select one alpha per outer fold, target type, and image input using four-fold `GroupKFold` inner validation by participant. Selection criterion: mean participant-balanced MSE averaged over the 12 standardized targets. Fit final outer-training model at the selected alpha with coordinate descent, `max_iter=50000`, `tol=1e-4`; save iteration count, dual gap, convergence status, and inner-validation loss for every alpha. Do not substitute another solver or silently accept non-convergence.
- **MLP:** one hidden layer of 128 ReLU units, AdamW, learning rate `1e-3`, weight decay `1e-4`, batch size 1,024; maximum 200 epochs, patience 15, minimum validation-loss improvement `1e-4`. For each outer fold, target type, and image input, use one fixed-seed inner validation split grouped by participant (20% of outer-training participants). Select the best epoch by participant-balanced validation MSE across the 12 standardized targets; refit a fresh model on all outer-training participants for that epoch count. Save training/validation loss curves and selected/stopped epochs. Record failures rather than substituting a configuration.
- **Fixed comparisons:** patient-mean reference; depth-only Ridge (`alpha=1`); colour-only Ridge (`alpha=1`, existing 12 RGB/HSV summary features); Elastic Net and MLP on spot embeddings; Elastic Net and MLP on spot embeddings plus depth. Ridge baselines are fitted only on outer-training participants.
- **Randomness:** preserve the Stage 1 fold assignments. Use fixed, recorded seeds derived from `20261112` for grouped inner splits, MLP initialization, and 10,000 participant bootstrap resamples. No search outside the stated grid or early-stopping budget.
