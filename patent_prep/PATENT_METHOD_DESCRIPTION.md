## Technical effect supported by the saved evidence

On the RENFAST test set, the U-Net had higher overlap with RENFAST expert-made masks than the fixed colour-threshold baseline. The family-macro paired Dice difference was `0.08699843983256006`, with primary filename-family bootstrap 95% CI `[0.07315876056020197, 0.09962074456605957]`; the secondary within-family image-bootstrap 95% CI was `[0.07856573117382186, 0.09553955260064735]`. See `PATENT_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md` and `D:\patent\stage2A_audit\PHASEA_final_metrics.json`.

This result is limited to agreement with the RENFAST masks and the saved filename-family split. It does not show medical accuracy, clinical utility, external generalization, or a validated tissue-level fibrosis percentage.
